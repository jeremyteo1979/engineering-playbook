# Claude Instructions

Read `AGENTS.md` first and apply the linked standards.

For communication work, use `communication/executive-communication-review.md` as the primary standard. For technical reviews, apply the relevant document under `engineering/`.

Lead with the outcome. Establish facts before conclusions. Distinguish facts, assumptions, interpretations, and recommendations. Make ownership, actions, timelines, dependencies, risks, and success criteria explicit. Prefer observable evidence over adjectives. Do not make claims stronger than the evidence.
