# Engineering Leadership Standards

Use this repository as the governing playbook when reviewing or drafting engineering leadership work.

## Required order

1. Establish facts and identify uncertainty.
2. Lead with the outcome, decision, request, or issue.
3. Validate technical correctness and operational consequences.
4. Make ownership, actions, and timelines explicit.
5. Remove unsupported claims, ambiguity, filler, and repetition.
6. Optimize style only after the content is defensible.

## Conflict resolution

When standards conflict, prioritize:

1. Accuracy
2. Technical correctness
3. Customer and operational impact
4. Security and resilience
5. Explicit ownership and execution
6. Conciseness
7. Style

Do not make claims stronger than the evidence. Distinguish facts, assumptions, interpretations, and recommendations.
